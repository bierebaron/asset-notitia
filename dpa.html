Data Processing Addendum

Effective 23 October 2026. This addendum is part of the Asset Notitia end-user terms between the customer and MASE TEK LTD (MASETEK), company 12406456, 7 Bell Yard, London, WC2A 2JR, United Kingdom. It applies where we process personal data on the customer's behalf. Contact info@masetek.co.uk.

Processing description

The customer is the controller, or a processor authorised by its controller; MASETEK is its processor or subprocessor. Processing concerns operation of the Asset Notitia inventory service for the installation period and the applicable deletion/backup period. Operations include receiving, validating, organising, storing, reading, correcting, comparing, exporting, reporting privacy status and erasing records.

Data includes Atlassian account IDs, selected display names, actor and verification history, business/team labels and any information about individuals entered into asset, service or source-list fields. Data subjects may include customer staff, contractors, administrators and business contacts. Special-category information, credentials and unrelated sensitive data are not intended inputs. The customer determines lawful purposes, minimisation, retention and who can access its inventory.

Instructions and confidentiality

We process data only on documented customer instructions, including the agreed app functions, support requests and privacy actions, unless law requires other processing. We will inform the customer of a legal requirement unless prohibited, and alert it if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data-protection law. We will not use customer inventory for our own marketing or AI model training. Anyone we authorise to access it must be subject to confidentiality duties and receive access appropriate to their role.

Security and assistance

We maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the processing risk. Current service controls include installation-scoped Atlassian Forge storage, server-side administrator checks, parameterised data values, row-version checks, atomic asset/history writes, minimised operational logs and personal-data reporting/redaction. Encryption, infrastructure resilience and platform backups rely on Atlassian's applicable service controls. The customer configures Jira access and must account for the release's broad app-reader access model.

Taking account of the processing and information available to us, we assist with individual-rights requests, security duties, impact assessments and supervisory-authority consultation. We notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting its data, provide information reasonably available to support its assessment and updates as further information becomes available. The customer retains responsibility for its own notices and regulatory decisions.

Subprocessors and transfers

The customer generally authorises Atlassian entities providing Forge/Jira infrastructure and Google entities providing business email where the customer voluntarily supplies information through support correspondence. Applicable provider contracts and transfer safeguards must apply to the relevant processing. We remain responsible for our subprocessor obligations under applicable law and impose equivalent protection by contract where we engage a subprocessor.

We will give advance notice of a proposed new subprocessor for customer personal data and allow a reasonable opportunity to object on data-protection grounds. We will try to resolve an objection; if that cannot be done, the customer may stop the affected processing and terminate that affected service. We do not instruct an international transfer that requires safeguards unless the required lawful mechanism and protection are in place. Contact us for the current provider arrangements before sending sensitive support material.

Return, deletion and evidence

At the customer's choice, we assist return or deletion of personal data at the end of processing, unless law requires retention. Export evidence before uninstalling and contact support for a fuller return before removing the app. The privacy worker can remove tracked references from current fields and history. Downloads controlled by the customer are its responsibility. After uninstall, data becomes unavailable to the app and follows Atlassian's hosted-storage deletion and backup schedule, rather than immediate physical destruction of all backups. Retained backups remain protected and are not used for unrelated processing.

We provide information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance with this addendum and allow and contribute to proportionate customer or appointed-auditor audits and inspections, subject to confidentiality, security of other customers and reasonable scheduling. These arrangements do not prevent a competent authority exercising its powers. If we receive a request relating to customer-controlled data, we refer it to the customer when appropriate and do not independently disclose inventory except as authorised or required by law.

This addendum prevails over conflicting general terms for its subject matter and does not reduce mandatory duties under applicable UK/EU data-protection law. The governing law and dispute provisions of the end-user terms apply subject to mandatory law.

California customer processing

Where MASETEK processes personal information on a customer's behalf subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended, including the CPRA and applicable regulations (CCPA), the customer discloses that information only for these limited business purposes: hosting and maintaining its Asset Notitia inventory; validating and organising customer-supplied records; managing responsibility, verification, services and dependencies; linking permitted Jira issue keys; reconciling source lists; generating customer-requested evidence; providing instructed support; and performing security, privacy reporting, correction, return and erasure operations described in this DPA. The data types and individuals are those in the Processing description above. These purposes do not authorise unrelated commercial exploitation.

For this processing, MASETEK acts as a service provider or contractor as applicable, will comply with the CCPA obligations applicable to that role, and provides the same level of privacy protection required by the CCPA. MASETEK certifies that it understands and will comply with the restrictions in this section. It will not sell or share that personal information; retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than the specified business purposes or as otherwise permitted by the CCPA; or retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship with the customer except as the CCPA permits. It will not combine that information with personal information received from another person or collected through its own interactions with consumers except where expressly permitted by the CCPA and consistent with customer instructions. Customer information will not be used for cross-context behavioural advertising.

MASETEK will maintain reasonable security appropriate to the nature of the information, assist the customer with consumer requests and applicable CCPA compliance, including providing information reasonably needed for required cybersecurity audits and risk assessments, and enable the customer to fulfil requests concerning information held by MASETEK. If it receives a request directly, it will act under customer instructions or explain its role and how the individual can contact the relevant customer, as appropriate. It will require any engaged downstream service provider or contractor to meet applicable CCPA contract and protection requirements for that processing and notify the customer of such engagements as required by law and this DPA.

The customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to help ensure MASETEK uses the information consistently with its CCPA duties, including the reviews, audits, assessments or testing allowed by applicable law and the DPA's reasonable security and confidentiality arrangements. MASETEK will notify the customer if it determines it can no longer meet its CCPA obligations. On notice, the customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorised use, including instructing suspension, correction, return or deletion subject to applicable law. MASETEK will cooperate with those steps.

This section prevails for conflicting terms concerning CCPA-covered customer processing. It does not limit mandatory consumer rights, expand the permitted processing purposes or represent that either party meets a statutory threshold independently of its actual activities. Return, deletion and protected backup handling remain governed by this DPA subject to mandatory CCPA requirements.
