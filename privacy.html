Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026. Asset Notitia is provided by MASE TEK LTD, trading as MASETEK, company number 12406456, registered in England and Wales at 7 Bell Yard, London, WC2A 2JR, United Kingdom. Contact info@masetek.co.uk for privacy or support requests.

Our role and the data we process

For inventory entered by an organisation, that organisation decides why and how personal data is used. We process it on the customer's instructions to provide Asset Notitia. Our Data Processing Addendum describes that relationship. We act as controller for our own business contact and support correspondence, using it to respond to requests, operate the service, maintain security and meet legal obligations.

The app stores customer-entered IT and OT records, locations, network identifiers, suppliers, lifecycle details, team labels, relationships, service memberships and linked Jira issue keys. Personal information can include Atlassian account IDs, selected users' display names, verification and change actors, timestamps and personal information customers enter into free-text fields or CSVs. Do not enter passwords, tokens, special-category data or unnecessary personal information.

The app reads Jira users to select and validate owners and custodians. It checks administrator permissions and issue visibility when linking work. It stores issue keys, not issue descriptions or attachments. It does not discover devices or create Jira issues in this release.

Why we use it

Processing enables inventory management, responsibility assignment, verification, lifecycle reviews, service/dependency linking, CSV reconciliation, evidence exports, security and privacy maintenance. We do not sell app data or use inventory for advertising or AI model training. For our own business correspondence we rely on legitimate interests in operating and supporting the service, and legal obligations where applicable. The customer is responsible for its own lawful basis and notices for inventory data.

Where data goes

App data is processed in Atlassian Forge functions and stored in Forge SQL, isolated by app installation. Jira data is accessed through Atlassian APIs. Stored account IDs and their data-age timestamps are reported to Atlassian's personal-data API to detect erasure or refresh requirements. No external application backend, app-authored analytics or external font service is configured. Fonts are bundled.

Evidence files are generated in the browser and saved to the user's device. They may contain personal information; the customer controls their subsequent sharing and retention. Support messages sent voluntarily to our business mailbox are handled through our Google-routed email service. Do not send an inventory or sensitive diagnostics by email unless a suitable process has been agreed.

Public documentation is hosted on Render. Delivery and security of those pages may involve connection information such as IP addresses and request details being processed by the hosting provider. No visitor analytics scripts or external font service are added to these pages. The app does not automatically send inventory to the documentation website. Contact info@masetek.co.uk about documentation-related privacy questions.

Atlassian and our communication/hosting providers operate internationally. Their applicable processing terms and transfer safeguards govern their processing. Customers with particular residency or transfer requirements should assess those arrangements before installation; we do not claim an independently certified residency badge or a UK-only processing commitment.

Access and security

Jira administrators manage inventory and privacy operations. Signed-in users with app access can read inventory, history, service/comparison records and linked issue keys and generate evidence. There are no granular OT-group or project-level read restrictions in this release. Use the app only where that access model is appropriate.

Controls include server-side administration checks, installation-scoped storage, bound SQL data values, row-version concurrency checks, atomic asset/history writes and sanitised operational error messages. These are controls, not a security certification or a guarantee that all risk is eliminated.

Retention and erasure

Inventory and change history remain in installation storage while retained by the customer; there is no automatic age-based inventory purge. Unlinking retains a change record. Audit history has a capacity guard that refuses further changes instead of silently trimming history.

Administrators can request erasure of tracked personal references. The worker removes tracked account IDs and name aliases from current records and historical diffs in batches; ownership may need reassignment. Writes pause while an erasure job is queued to avoid reintroducing references. The worker runs every five minutes, with an administrator control to advance processing. Completion time depends on data volume and retries. Contact us about untracked free-text information or an outstanding queue.

Erasure does not delete Atlassian accounts, Jira issues, downloads already held by customers or separate support correspondence. After uninstall, Atlassian applies its hosted-storage retention and backup lifecycle; deletion is not immediate physical destruction of every platform backup. See Atlassian's current data lifecycle.

We retain ordinary support correspondence as needed for the case and for up to 12 months after closure, unless a legal obligation or active dispute requires longer. Requested diagnostic copies should be minimised and deleted after their purpose is complete.

Your rights and contact

Ask your organisation's administrator about inventory access, correction, erasure or export. You may also contact info@masetek.co.uk; we will identify the appropriate controller and assist it. For information we control, applicable rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. We may need to verify identity and authority before acting. Requests are handled within applicable legal timescales. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your competent supervisory authority.

Material changes will be dated here and communicated through the listing or an appropriate service channel. This policy covers Asset Notitia and its documentation, not Atlassian's services or other MASETEK services.
